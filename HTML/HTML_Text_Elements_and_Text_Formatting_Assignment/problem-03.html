City Launches New Digital Learning Center

Community students receive access to computers and learning resources

A new digital learning center was opened on 25 August 2026 to provide students with access to computers, internet-based learning resources, and technology workshops.

The center will provide free access to its basic learning facilities for registered students. Organizers said the facility would focus on practical learning, programming, digital literacy, and online research skills.

Teachers and volunteers will conduct introductory sessions during the first few weeks. The organizers described the project as an important step toward making digital education more accessible.
