GLA University

About My College


GLA University, Greater Noida is a dynamic and forward-thinking campus located in the premier educational hub of Knowledge Park II, National Capital Region (NCR). Designed explicitly as an industry-integrated learning center, the campus stands out as a pioneer in modern education, holding the distinction of being North India's first Google Agentic AI University and India's first Microsoft GenAI Campus. This cutting-edge infrastructure allows students to learn within an ecosystem driven by artificial intelligence, smart classrooms, and advanced technology laboratories.The institution offers future-ready academic programs, including Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and AI/ML, Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), and Master of Business Administration (MBA). All courses are delivered by experienced faculty members who prioritize personalized mentorship and practical, hands-on learning. Beyond its innovative academic curriculum, the campus is recognized for its powerful corporate interface and exceptional placement record--boasting a highest career package of ₹60 LPA alongside recruitment partnerships with global industry giants such as Amazon, TCS, and Microsoft.

Course Offered


GLA University, Greater Noida offers industry-focused engineering and management programs. Undergraduates can pursue a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, with specialised tracks in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML). The campus also offers a professional BBA program for undergraduate business students. For postgraduate studies, the institution provides an AI-integrated MBA and an advanced MCA degree. Every course focuses on practical tech applications and corporate readiness to prepare students for the modern job market.

Campus Life


Campus life at GLA University, Greater Noida balances academic focus with professional networking and interactive activities. Located in a bustling educational hub, the campus features a modern, corporate-style infrastructure designed to encourage collaboration. Students can participate in various student-led clubs, tech hackathons, cultural festivals, and professional seminars that build leadership and communication skills. The campus also provides comprehensive facilities, including sports zones, cafeteria spaces, high-speed Wi-Fi networks, and well-equipped technology labs to support a balanced student routine.
