Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must follow institutional instructions and maintain respectful behavior on campus.

Students must carry their identification card when attending classes and academic activities.

Attendance

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Attendance requirements may vary according to the academic program and institutional policy.

Examination

Students must follow all examination instructions and must not use unauthorized material.

Students should report to the examination room at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted before the announced deadline.
Late submissions may require additional approval.

Example deadline: 11:59 PM on the stated submission date.

Discipline

Students must behave responsibly and respect teachers, staff, classmates, and college property.

Disciplinary action may be taken when institutional rules are intentionally violated.

Important Notice

Always read official notices carefully because dates, procedures, and academic requirements can change.
