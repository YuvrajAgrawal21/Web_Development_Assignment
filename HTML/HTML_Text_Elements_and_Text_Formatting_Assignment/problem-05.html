Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is represented by H2O.

Carbon dioxide is represented by CO2.

Oxygen gas is represented by O2.

Sulfuric acid is represented by H2SO4.

Ammonia is represented by NH3.

Chemical formula describes the elements and their proportions in a compound.

Physics

Area is measured in square units such as m2.

Volume is measured in cubic units such as m3.

Scientific notation can represent values such as 103 and 10-6.

Acceleration describes the rate at which velocity changes with time.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to produce food.

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.

DNA contains genetic information and carries biological instructions.

Quick Revision

H2O is water, CO2 is carbon dioxide, m2 represents area, and m3 represents volume.
