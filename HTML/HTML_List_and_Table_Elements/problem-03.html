Introduction

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering focuses on programming, software development, databases, web technologies and emerging areas of computer science. Students are encouraged to develop both theoretical knowledge and practical skills.

Steps Before a Practical Class

  1. Check the practical schedule and laboratory number.
  2. Read the practical instructions before entering the lab.
  3. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  4. Log in to the assigned computer system.
  5. Complete the practical task according to the instructions.
  6. Save your work and submit it to the faculty member.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must carry their identity card.
  • Maintain discipline inside classrooms and laboratories.
  • Do not use mobile phones during lectures without permission.
  • Keep classrooms and laboratories clean.
  • Submit assignments before the given deadline.

Important Academic Notice: Internal assessments will be conducted according to the department schedule.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students apply concepts using computers or equipment.
Tutorial
A guided academic session focused on solving questions and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
An evaluation conducted during the semester to measure student progress.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity designed to develop practical skills.
