GLA University

Admission Overview

Admissions at GLA University operate primarily on an entrance-based model, anchored by the university’s in-house online entrance test known as the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), alongside accepted national-level exam scores. The structured application cycle typically runs from January through July, requiring prospective candidates to first register online, verify academic eligibility such as achieving a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 for undergraduate programs or graduation for postgraduate studies, and pay an application fee. Following the online entrance test—or by submitting alternative national scores like JEE Main for engineering or CAT/MAT for management—shortlisted applicants clear personal interviews or group discussions where applicable, after which the university releases prompt results within 24 hours, concluding the workflow with document verification and token fee payments to lock in seat allocations.

Eligibility
The eligibility framework at GLA University primarily demands a baseline of 50% aggregate marks in the qualifying examinations for most general undergraduate and postgraduate streams. Specifically, for undergraduate engineering programs (B.Tech), candidates must pass their 10+2 examinations with a minimum of 50% marks overall, with mandatory subjects including Physics and Mathematics combined with Chemistry or Computer Science. For postgraduate management programs (MBA) and computer applications (MCA), applicants must hold a recognized three-year Bachelor’s degree with at least 50% marks in any discipline, while specialized or honours variants like MBA (Hons) can require up to 65% aggregate marks. Furthermore, a standard 5% relaxation threshold on academic scores is extended across most courses for candidates belonging to SC/ST categories, and all qualifying criteria must be fulfilled alongside clearing the university's mandatory GLAET online entrance exam or presenting valid national-level test cut-offs

Admission Process

  1. Online Registration:Online Registration: Create an account on the official GLA admission portal and fill in personal, contact, and academic details.
  2. Fee Payment:Fee Payment: Pay the application fee digitally using a credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI to activate the application form.
  3. Slot Booking: Log in to the portal to book a preferred date, time, and test center for the university's online entrance exam.
  4. Entrance Screening: Appear for the computer-based GLAET exam, or skip it by submitting valid national test scores like JEE Main, CUET, CAT, or MAT.
  5. Personal Interview: Attend a personal interview or group discussion round, which is mandatory for specific postgraduate and doctoral programs.
  6. Merit Declaration: Check the online portal for selection results, which the university typically releases within 24 hours of the entrance exam.
  7. Seat Allotment: Pay the initial advance token fee online to reserve and lock the allocated seat in the chosen course.
  8. Document Verification: Present original academic transcripts, identity proofs, and migration certificates at the campus counter for physical validation.
  9. Final Enrollment: Submit the remaining semester tuition fees and obtain the official university enrollment number and student ID card.

Required Documents

The mandatory documentation framework at GLA University requires candidates to submit a comprehensive set of academic and personal records during the final verification phase.For academic validation, applicants must provide original and self-attested photocopies of their Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and passing certificates, alongside graduation degree certificates and semester transcripts for postgraduate programs. To establish identity and personal background, the university mandates the submission of a valid government-issued photo ID (such as an Aadhaar Card or Passport), recent passport-sized photographs, and a category certificate if claiming SC/ST/OBC seat reservations or score relaxations. Finally, for institutional transfer and character assessment, students must furnish an official Migration Certificate and Transfer Certificate (TC) from their previously attended board or university, a Character Certificate signed by the head of the last institution, and, if applicable, an official Gap Certificate affidavit explaining any breaks in continuous education.

Important Terms

The core vocabulary and critical acronyms defining the GLA University admissions framework include:

  • GLAET: The university’s proprietary online computer-based entrance examination required for final selection.

  • Aggregate Percentage: The cumulative score across all subjects in qualifying exams used to verify eligibility.

  • Seat Allotment Fee: An advance token payment required to lock and secure a course seat

  • Document Verification: The administrative phase where physical original certificates are verified against digital submissions.

  • Score Relaxation: A 5% lowering of the academic eligibility cutoff for SC/ST reserved categories.

  • Gap Affidavit: A legal stamp-paper document explaining any chronological breaks in a student's education.

  • Migration Certificate: A clearance document from the previous education board allowing enrollment at GLA.
