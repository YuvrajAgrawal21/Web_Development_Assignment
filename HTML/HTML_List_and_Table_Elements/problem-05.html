Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of talent, creativity, technology, sports and entertainment. Students from different departments can participate in exciting competitions and showcase their skills.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 brings students together through cultural activities, technical competitions, sports and creative events. The fest provides an opportunity for students to learn, compete, collaborate and create memorable experiences.

Registration Process

  1. Choose the competition you want to participate in.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements and competition rules.
  3. Fill out the registration form with correct details.
  4. Submit the registration form before the deadline.
  5. Report to the venue at least 30 minutes before the event.

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report to the venue on time.
  • Registration after the deadline will not be accepted.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Any form of unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain discipline throughout the fest.
  • College property must be handled responsibly.
  • The decision of the judges will be considered final.

Important Event Terms

Open Event
An event that allows participation from students without restricting participation to a specific department.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants work together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing and organizing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final positions.
