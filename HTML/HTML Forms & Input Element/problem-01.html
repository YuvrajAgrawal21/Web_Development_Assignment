College Admission Form

The admission form of GLA University can be filled out online through the university’s admission portal. First, the student needs to register by providing basic details such as name, mobile number, email ID, and the desired course. After registration, the student logs in and fills in personal, academic, and contact details. The student then uploads the required documents and photographs, selects the preferred course, and pays the application fee online. After checking all the information carefully, the student submits the application form and saves or downloads the confirmation for future reference.
