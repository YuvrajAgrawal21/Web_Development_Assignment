Attendee Preparation Tips

  • Bring an updated resume.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Carry your student ID.
  • Wear suitable formal or smart-casual clothing.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Check in at the entrance.
  3. Explore company booths.
  4. Attend workshops and talks.
  5. Meet recruiters.
  6. Complete the feedback form.

Important Terms

Career Expo
An event where students meet companies and explore careers.
Internship
A temporary work experience opportunity for students.
Placement
An employment opportunity provided by a company.
Workshop
An interactive session focused on practical learning.
Certification
A credential showing completion of a learning program.
Networking
Building professional connections with other people.
