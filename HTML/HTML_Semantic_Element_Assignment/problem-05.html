Event Overview

The National Career and Education Fair is an event where students can meet companies, colleges and different organizations.

Students can learn about jobs, internships, scholarships and higher education opportunities.

The main purpose of this event is to help students understand different career options.

Registration is compulsory for students who want to attend the event.

Objectives of the Event

  • Help students find suitable career options.
  • Provide information about different companies.
  • Tell students about internships and jobs.
  • Provide information about scholarships.
  • Connect students with recruiters and colleges.
"The right information at the right time can help a student choose a better career." — Event Organizer

Preparation Guide

Students should prepare properly before coming to the career fair.

  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Research the companies before visiting.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Wear clean and suitable clothes.
  • Carry your college ID card.
  • Prepare some questions for recruiters.

What Students Should Do

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Reach the venue on time.
  3. Attend useful sessions.
  4. Visit company stalls.
  5. Talk to recruiters.
  6. Note down useful information.

Important Notice

Students should reach the venue at least 30 minutes before their selected session.

Some sessions may have limited seats.

Bring your registration confirmation with you.

Important Terms

Career Fair
An event where students meet companies and learn about career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary work or learning opportunity for gaining practical experience.
Placement
A process through which students get employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial support given to eligible students.
Apprenticeship
A training program where a person learns practical skills through work.
