New Digital Learning Center Opens in Noida

Students receive access to computers and digital learning resources

A new digital learning center was opened in noida on 26 September 2026 to provide students with access to computers and educational resources.

The center will provide free access to basic digital learning facilities for registered students. The facility is designed to help students improve their computer and technology skills.

The center will conduct introductory workshops covering programming, digital literacy, online research, and basic computer applications. These sessions are expected to help students become more comfortable with technology.

The project was created because many students need additional opportunities to practice digital skills outside the classroom. The organizers believe that practical learning can improve student confidence and career preparation.
