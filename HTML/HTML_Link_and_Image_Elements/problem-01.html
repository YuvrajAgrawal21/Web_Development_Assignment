GLA University

About

GLA University, located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, is a prestigious private institution accredited with an 'A+' grade by NAAC. Established in 2010, the university offers a wide range of UGC-approved undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. It focuses heavily on academic excellence, practical skill development, and strong industry placements.

Admissions for the new academic session are now open.

Programs

The university offers programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Administration, Commerce, Mathematics, and other academic areas. Students receive both theoretical knowledge and practical learning opportunities.

Campus Life

Campus life includes academic activities, seminars, cultural events, sports, workshops, and student projects. Students can participate in activities that help develop teamwork and communication skills.
